04. Forensic Signals

The Signatures Deepfakes Cannot Hide

Mathematical, physical, and optical anomalies exploited by our detector

1. GAN Checkerboard Artifacts (FFT Analysis)

Spectral

Transposed convolutions (deconvolutions) in GAN generators cause periodic spatial stride overlaps, visible as prominent artificial high-frequency frequency peaks under 2D Fourier power spectra.

2. Photo-Response Non-Uniformity (PRNU)

Physical

Genuine camera sensors have physical pixel sensitivity variances (Bayer demosaicing photon shot noise). Deepfakes feature synthetically smooth skin with zero natural sensor noise.

3. Boundary Seam Feathering & Blending

Spatial

Autoencoders paste swapped faces onto target host bodies, creating color temperature mismatches and edge smoothing along perioral lines, jawlines, and ear perimeters.

4. Temporal Motion & Blinking Rhythm

Temporal

Deepfake models generate frames independently or with minimal sequence memory, resulting in unnatural blinking rates, micro-jitter, and optical flow vector discrepancies.
