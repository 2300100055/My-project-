# Privacy Policy — Verity

**Plain Language. No legal jargon.**

---

## What We Analyze

You upload an image or video. We analyze it for signs of AI generation or manipulation. That's it.

## What We Store

- Your file is stored temporarily under a randomly generated ID (not your name, email, or IP address).
- We store the analysis result so you can retrieve it.
- **We delete your file and results automatically after 60 minutes.**
- You can also delete them immediately using the delete button in the result view.

## What We Do NOT Store

- We do not store your name, email, or any account information (no login required).
- We do not log the contents of your files.
- We do not retain your IP address in our result logs.
- We do not use your uploads to train any AI model, ever.
- We do not share your uploads with third parties.

## What We Log

We keep operational logs for security and reliability:
- Timestamp of request
- File type and size (not contents)
- Random job ID
- Error messages (no file contents)

Logs are retained for 30 days for security monitoring, then deleted.

## Cookies and Tracking

- No tracking cookies.
- No analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.).
- No fingerprinting.
- We use a single session cookie only if you are logged in (login is optional and not required for analysis).

## Your Rights

- **Delete:** Click "Delete my results" in the result view, or they auto-delete in 60 minutes.
- **Access:** Your result is available via its random job ID for 60 minutes.
- **Data portability:** Download your analysis as JSON or PDF at any time during the retention window.

## Third-Party Services

- Verity uses no third-party analytics, ad networks, or social login.
- If you use the Docker image, your data stays on your own infrastructure.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
